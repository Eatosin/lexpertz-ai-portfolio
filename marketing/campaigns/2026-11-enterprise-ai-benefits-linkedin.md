89% of enterprises use AI. 37% can attribute any EBIT impact to it.

That gap is now the job.

McKinsey's 2026 survey puts individual productivity gains at 80%. Grant Thornton finds firms with fully integrated AI are almost 4x as likely to report revenue growth as those still piloting. The benefits are real. The averages are not your business case.

The benefits that survive an audit look like cost per claim, end-to-end cycle time, first-pass yield, quote turnaround, and the share of decisions a reviewer can defend. The ones that fail are the averages. "80% of employees are more productive" with no baseline, no comparison, and no evidence trail is a survey result, not a business case.

The first win is usually capacity: the submissions, claims, or tickets the team can now take on.

Audit readiness is the multiplier. 78% of executives lack strong confidence they could pass an independent AI governance audit in 90 days. The firms that can produce evidence are also the ones reporting the growth.

I wrote the seven benefits, the evidence behind each, and a 90-day proof plan. Link in the comments.
